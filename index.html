// PLANETARY ARCHAEOLOGY & THE ARTEMIS ACCORDS

Not Space Trash—Time Capsules of Humanity’s First Steps Beyond Earth

Over 200,000 kilograms of human-made hardware rest on the Moon and Mars. Unlike terrestrial monuments eroded by rain and tectonic plates, Apollo descent stages and Martian rovers exist in near-eternal preservation—shielded by vacuum or gently blanketed in iron-oxide silt.

ACTIVE PASSIVE SCIENCE

Apollo 11, 14, and 15 Laser Retroreflectors still bounce Earth laser pulses back every night, proving the Moon spirals away from Earth by 3.8 cm per year.

FUTURE ASTROBIOLOGY & MATERIALS LABS

When future astronauts visit Opportunity or Ingenuity, studying decades of cosmic-ray and dust exposure on their hulls will teach us how to build permanent cities on Mars.

ENVIRONMENTAL DECAY VECTORS NSSDCA REF
LUNAR VACUUM (APOLLO / LRV) ΔT: +120°C to -130°C

No wind or rain; kapton foil experiences solar UV embrittlement and micrometeorite gardening.

MARTIAN SURFACE (OPPORTUNITY / INSIGHT) TAU OPACITY: 0.4 – 10.8

Electrostatic 2-micron iron-oxide dust settles on solar panels unless swept by seasonal convective vortices (dust devils).

HELIOCENTRIC ORBIT (KEPLER / SPITZER) 1.01 – 1.70 AU SOLAR DRIFT

Passivated transmitters; spacecraft remain stable in solar orbit for millions of years without atmospheric drag.
