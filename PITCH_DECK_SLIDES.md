## SLIDE 2: The Problem

### **The Unsung Monuments of Space Exploration**
#### *Decades of historic hardware sit forgotten in cosmic silence.*

- **The Reality of Space Exploration:**
  - Over the past 57 years, dozens of landers, rovers, and orbital probes have been left behind on the Moon, Mars, and in heliocentric orbit—totaling over **200,000 kg** of historic human engineering.
- **The Core Problem (Pain Point):**
  - **Public Amnesia:** Public attention focuses almost exclusively on *active* missions (like *Perseverance*, *JWST*, or *Artemis*).
  - **Silent Pioneers:** Legendary trailblazers—such as *Opportunity*, *InSight*, *Viking 1 & 2*, and the *Apollo Lunar Modules*—stand abandoned in vacuum or buried under Martian dust.
  - **Fragmented Data:** Official technical records inside scientific catalogs are dry, scattered, and disconnected from the human emotion and historic sacrifice behind each machine.

> **Speaker Notes:**  
> "Everyone knows about active missions, but what happens when a rover’s solar panels are choked by Martian dust? Machines like Opportunity and InSight become silent monuments, and their data remains locked in complex archives."

## SLIDE 3: Our Solution

### **Introducing "Artifacts of Silence"**
#### *Where official NASA telemetry meets emotional first-person storytelling.*

- **What We Built:**
  - An immersive, futuristic web application that bridges **official NASA open archival data (NSSDCA)** with **intimate first-person monologues** told from the perspective of the abandoned machines themselves.
- **Key Platform Features:**
  1. **Interactive Celestial Filtering:** Instant switching across **[All]**, **[The Moon]**, **[Mars]**, and **[Deep Space / Orbit]**.
  2. **The Voice of the Machine:** Emotional first-person narratives + built-in Web Speech audio narration.
  3. **Chrono-Regolith Time-Lapse (1969–2026):** Interactive slider simulating 57 years of Martian dust accumulation and mission status transitions.
  4. **Complete NSSDCA Dossiers:** Structured technical specs, landing coordinates, left-behind instruments, and archival galleries.

> **Speaker Notes:**  
> "Our solution, *Artifacts of Silence*, transforms raw NASA catalog numbers into living history. Users can filter by celestial body, scrub through a 1969–2026 dust time-lapse, and hear each spacecraft tell its own story."

## SLIDE 4: Data & Architecture

### **Powered by NASA NSSDCA Data**
#### *Authentic scientific metadata built on a fast, modern web stack.*

- **Official NASA Data Sources:**
  - Built directly on authentic designators, coordinates, and payload manifests from the **NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA)** and **NASA/JPL Photojournal**.
- **8 Comprehensive Relic Dossiers Across 3 Domains:**
  - **The Moon (2):** *Apollo 11 Eagle Descent Stage* (`1969-059C`) & *Apollo 15–17 Lunar Roving Vehicles* (`LRV-1/2/3`).
  - **Mars (4):** *Sojourner* (`1996-068A`), *Spirit & Opportunity* (`2003-027A/032A`), *Viking 1 & 2* (`1975-075C/083C`), and *InSight & Phoenix* (`2018-042A` / `2007-034A`).
  - **Deep Space / Orbit (2):** *Dawn Spacecraft at Ceres* (`2007-043A`) & *Kepler Space Telescope* (`2009-011A`).
- **Clean, Zero-Lag Tech Stack:**
  - **HTML5 + Tailwind CSS + Alpine.js** for instantaneous reactivity and mobile responsiveness.
  - **Three.js WebGL Engine** for a 60fps interactive 3D starfield and planetary telemetry wireframe.
